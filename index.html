मलाई एक कप चिया खुवाउनुहोस् Get paid in chiya, not paperwork.

Buy Me a Coffee pays out through Stripe, which doesn't work in Nepal. So this isn't a platform — it's a free tool that turns your eSewa, Khalti or bank details into a page you can share. Supporters pay you directly. Nobody takes a cut, because nobody is in the middle.

कसरी चल्छThree steps.
No forms in triplicate.

There's nothing to sign up for. You fill in a form, you get a link, the link is your page.

१

Fill in four boxes

Your name, one line about what you make, the price of a chiya, and where people can pay you.

२

Get your link

Your whole page is packed into the URL itself. Nothing is sent to us — there's no us to send it to.

३

Put it in your bio

Supporters open it, copy your eSewa, Khalti or bank details, and send the money from their own app. You get 100%.

यस्तो देखिन्छThis is the whole thing.

One page, your emoji, your prices, your payment details. Supporters copy your number or scan your Fonepay QR and send it from their own banking app — the same way they'd pay a friend. It's an instructions page, not a checkout: no card form, no redirect, nothing to go wrong in the middle.

किन स्थानीयThe foreign platforms can't pay you.

It isn't a bug you can work around with a VPN. Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi and Patreon all settle through Stripe or PayPal, and neither supports payouts to Nepal. Creators here end up borrowing a relative's foreign account, or giving up. This starts from the other end: your own account, from the first rupee.

What this is not

There's no company, no wallet, no balance and no database. Nothing tracks who paid you — if you want a record, that's your bank statement. Your link is the only copy of your page, so keep it somewhere safe. Lose it and you just make a new one.
